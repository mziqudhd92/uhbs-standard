# AEP Reporting

## Artifacts

Keep AEP outputs **separate** from the UHQS scorecard:

| Artifact | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `SCORECARD.json` / report | Normative UHQS (unchanged) |
| `advanced-evidence.json` | Machine-verifiable AEP addendum |
| `ADVANCED-EVIDENCE.md` | Analyst-readable methodology, results, limitations |

Schemas:

- [`aep-experiment.schema.json`](https://github.com/mziqudhd92/uhbs-standard/blob/main/schemas/aep-experiment.schema.json)
- [`aep-trial.schema.json`](https://github.com/mziqudhd92/uhbs-standard/blob/main/schemas/aep-trial.schema.json)
- [`advanced-evidence.schema.json`](https://github.com/mziqudhd92/uhbs-standard/blob/main/schemas/advanced-evidence.schema.json)

## Interpretation wording

**Preferred:**

> AEP evidence observed … under the declared controlled conditions.

**Forbidden:**

> UHBS-certified attacker engagement / proven against all attackers / AEP grade replaces UHQS.

Always state `uhqs_unchanged: true`.

## Status examples

### Positive (`valid`)

Primary outcome computed with adequate samples and passing evaluator control.
Example: DTDR ≈ 2.2 with n≥5 per arm and `control_status=passed`.

### Null / weak effect (`valid` with interval crossing indifference)

Metric computed, but confidence interval includes no practical effect. Still
`valid` if design requirements were met — report the interval honestly.

### Inconclusive

Too few trials, undefined KM median under heavy censoring, or missing detector
rows for FSV layers.

### Control failed (`control_failed`)

Evaluator control trials mark `evaluator_control_passed: false`. Capability-linked
metrics are downgraded; do not claim attacker-capability conclusions.

## Banner (required in Markdown)

Every Markdown addendum includes an informative-only, **lab/sandbox** banner
generated by `uhbs aep report`, plus a short academic-credit block (Zhu 2019;
Collins et al. 2024; Ersok et al. 2022; Li et al. 2020) with the statement that
citation does not imply endorsement. Full ledger:
[Research foundations & credits](research-foundations.md).
